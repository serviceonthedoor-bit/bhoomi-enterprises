Why book with Bhoomi

One workshop, every drivetrain — so you're not sent elsewhere halfway through a job.

  • EV and ICE vehicles handled in the same workshop, by the same team, so mixed fleets don't need two garages.
  • Every job starts with inspection and diagnosis — you get an estimate before any work begins.
  • Spare parts and batteries are stocked on site, so most repairs don't wait on outside sourcing.
  • Separate, dedicated bays for battery handling and EV diagnostics, apart from general mechanical work.
